The problem

Busy isn't the same as balanced

You're not short on effort. You get up, get through the list, fall into bed. But at the end of the month something nags at you: the gym you meant to get back to, the friend you haven't rung, the savings goal you set in January and haven't looked at since.

That's not a willpower problem. It's a visibility problem.

Most planners are built around tasks, and most of your tasks come from work. So the loudest part of your life gets all the space on the page, and the quiet parts slip without anyone noticing — including you.

Maybe you've tried to fix it already: a Notion setup that took a weekend to build and a week to abandon, a habit app you stopped opening. You don't need a more complicated system. You need to see your whole life in one place.

The RBGM method

Plan by the roles you play, not the tasks you have

You're not just "someone with a to-do list". You're a professional, a parent or partner, a friend — someone with a body to look after, money to manage and, ideally, some fun to have. Choose 8 areas of your life and name them however suits you. Every goal and habit belongs to one of them, and the dashboard tracks each area on its own.

Goals

Up to 100 goals, each with a home

Pick a life area, write how you'll measure it, add dates and say whether it's urgent and important. The sheet then:

Priorities

Your Eisenhower matrix, filled in for you

Every open goal lands in the right box automatically, with a chart showing where your attention is going. If everything is "urgent", you'll see it.

Weekly Planner

Type any date and the week lays itself out. Up to 15 tasks a day; tick them and they're struck through. Every day has its own progress bar, and three charts show how the week is going.

Calendar

Quarterly Review

The 10-minute reset

FAQ

Questions

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes — in Excel for Mac as well as Windows. It uses formulas only (no macros), so there's nothing to enable and no security warnings. It doesn't work in Apple Numbers.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. You get a Google Sheets edition as a "Make a copy" link — one click puts your own private copy in your Google Drive.

Which version of Excel do I need?

Any recent desktop version of Excel, including one-off purchases like Office 2019, 2021 and 2024, as well as Microsoft 365. Microsoft 365 users can also use the bonus edition with native checkboxes.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay once and the files are yours. Reuse them every year by changing the year in Settings.

I'm not great with spreadsheets. Will I cope?

You won't write a formula or build a chart. You type goals, habits and tasks; the sheet does the rest. The Start Here tab takes about 10 minutes, and the example file shows what it looks like in use.

Can I change the life areas and habits?

Yes. Rename all 8 life areas and all 12 habits in Settings and the whole file updates to match.

Can I use it on my phone?

It's designed for a computer, where the dashboard has room to breathe. It isn't designed or tested for phones.

What's your refund policy?

It's an instant digital download, so sales are generally final. But if it doesn't open or work as described on your version of Excel or Google Sheets, email us within 14 days and we'll fix it or refund you.
